Ziba - How Ziba Connects People

Ziba's matching is a set of clear rules, applied the same way to everyone. This page explains them in plain language: no secret formula, and no claims we cannot back up.

1. Profiles come first

Every profile on Ziba is a full page: photos, written prompt answers, and optional voice prompts you can listen to. You swipe through the whole profile as one page, so a like is based on the person, not on a single photo. To appear in discovery, a profile needs at least 3 approved photos, and every member's email is verified before the account exists.

2. Preferences are checked both ways

Matching on Ziba is mutual. You set who you are interested in, and the people you see have also said they are interested in someone like you. If either side's preferences rule the other out, you simply do not appear to each other. The same applies to people you have passed, blocked, or reported: they leave your deck, and you leave theirs where the rules say so.

3. Age ranges apply in both directions

Your age range is a real boundary, not a suggestion. You see people inside your range, and you are only shown to people whose range includes your age. If a profile seems to be missing, a range that does not overlap in both directions is the most common reason. Ranges can be changed at any time in Filters.

4. Distance

With your permission, Ziba uses your approximate location to show how far away someone is and to apply the distance limit you set, from nearby to worldwide. Your exact location is never shown to anyone. Distance filtering is free for every member.

5. Your intention is shown, never used as a wall

Your intention (for example, dating or a relationship) appears on your profile and counts toward the match percentage. It never hides anyone from you by itself. In a small community, hard walls based on a single answer would leave everyone with an empty deck, so Ziba shows the information and lets you decide.

6. What the match percentage counts

Some profiles show a match percentage. It counts how many of your answers line up with the other person's answers, across your onboarding answers and profile details such as religion, smoking, drinking, children, and family plans. Answers marked "prefer not to say" are left out on both sides. The percentage appears only when the two of you share enough answers (at least 8) for the number to mean something; below that, no percentage is shown rather than a made-up one. It is a reading guide, not a prediction: a high percentage cannot promise chemistry, and a low one cannot rule it out.

7. A chat opens only when both people say yes

Nobody can message you out of nowhere on Ziba. A conversation starts only after two people have liked each other. The same idea runs Social: friend requests go to people who can accept or decline, and friendships form only when the other side accepts.

8. Why free likes have a limit

Free accounts get 7 dating likes and 7 Social friend requests per rolling 12 hours. The limit exists so that a like means something: people read profiles and choose, instead of liking everyone and deciding later. Ziba Premium removes the limits. Paid accounts do not get shown to more people because they paid; Premium changes your limits and tools, not other people's decks.

9. What Ziba does not do

  • No paid placement in your deck. Nobody can pay to be shown to you.
  • No selling access to specific people. Any profile you can see, you can like with a normal like.
  • No claims about a learning algorithm. The rules above are the whole system, and when they change, this page changes.

Ziba - Frequently Asked Questions

Short answers about matching, limits, Premium, verification, safety, and your account. For anything else, email Ziba.dating@gmail.com.

Getting started

How do I sign up?
Enter your email address and we send you a verification code. Your account is created only after you enter the code, and then you answer the profile questions. The same code check applies the first time you sign in with Google or Apple. If you already have a verified account, signing in takes you straight into the app, with no code and no questions again.

Who can join?
Adults only. You must be 18 or older, and you may hold one account.

What do I need before people can see me?
A completed profile with at least 3 approved photos. Profiles with fewer photos are not shown in discovery.

Matching

How does matching work?
You see profiles one at a time and swipe through the whole profile. When two people like each other, it is a match, and only then can you chat. Preferences are checked both ways: you only see people whose preferences you also fit. The full explanation is on our How Ziba Connects People page.

What is the match percentage?
A score on a profile that counts how many of your answers line up with theirs, across your onboarding answers and profile details. It appears only when the two of you share enough answers to make it meaningful. It is a guide for reading a profile, not a promise about compatibility.

Why am I not seeing someone, or why are they not seeing me?
Common reasons: your age ranges do not overlap in both directions, the distance is beyond the limit one of you set, one of you has paused your account, or one profile does not have 3 approved photos yet. Widening your age range or distance usually fixes it.

Can I change my answers and preferences later?
Yes. Every onboarding answer and every preference can be changed in the app at any time, and changes save to your account.

Free limits

What is free?
Ziba is free to use, with two separate limits: 7 dating likes per rolling 12 hours and 7 Social friend requests per rolling 12 hours. The age, intention, and distance filters are free for everyone. Voice messages in chat are free for everyone to send.

What happens when I run out of likes?
The app tells you that you are out of likes and offers Premium for more. Your remaining count is shown on the Likes page. Likes come back as the 12-hour window moves.

Ziba Premium and billing

What is Ziba Premium?
One subscription tier with everything: unlimited likes and friend requests, all filters, Rewind (Bring back), Super Likes, Boost, Incognito, Travel mode, seeing everyone who liked you in full, your previous matches, your passed-people list, and sending photos and videos in chat.

How much does it cost?
$4.99 per week, $15.00 per month, or $40.00 every 3 months. The App Store shows the exact price in your region before you confirm.

How am I billed?
By Apple, through your App Store account. Ziba never sees or stores your card details. Subscriptions renew automatically until you cancel, and when you cancel, Premium stays active until the end of the period you already paid for.

How do I cancel?
In iOS Settings, under Subscriptions. Find Ziba and turn off renewal.

Does deleting the app or my account cancel my subscription?
No. Deleting the app, or deleting your Ziba account, does not cancel a subscription with Apple. To stop being charged, cancel in iOS Settings, under Subscriptions.

I paid but Premium is not showing. What now?
Open the Premium page in the app and tap Restore Purchases. If it still does not appear, email Ziba.dating@gmail.com.

Can I get a refund?
App Store purchases are refunded by Apple under Apple's rules. Request it through Apple's report-a-problem page or your purchase history.

Verification

What is the face check?
An optional in-app check that compares a selfie with your profile photos, to show the photos appear to be the same person. It does not limit anything in the app: your account works the same without it.

What is the "ID checked" badge?
It means the member completed the optional in-app ID check, which reads a government ID shown to the camera. We store the verification status, not a copy of the ID. The badge means the check was completed. It is not a background check and not a guarantee of anyone's identity or safety. See the Safety Center for what our checks do and do not prove.

Safety

How do I report or block someone?
Report from their profile, from inside your chat, or in Social. Block is on the same menus. Blocking is immediate and two-way: you stop seeing each other everywhere in Ziba. Reports are reviewed by Ziba's operator, and a chat is opened for review only when it has been reported.

What happens after I report?
We review the report, the profiles involved, and the relevant chat. Confirmed violations follow the enforcement ladder in the Community Guidelines: warning, content removal, suspension, or a ban.

My account was warned, suspended, or banned. Can I appeal?
Yes. Email Ziba.dating@gmail.com from the email address on your account and explain what happened. A person reads every appeal, and we tell you the reason for the decision and the outcome of the review.

Someone is asking me for money. What should I do?
Stop, do not send anything, and report them. Asking for money is banned on Ziba in all cases. More patterns are listed in the Safety Center.

Your account

How do I delete my account?
In the app: Profile, then Settings, then delete your account. Deleting locks the account immediately, and your account and profile data are deleted within 30 days. Records of reports and moderation actions are kept for 90 days to enforce our safety rules, then deleted. You can also email us to request deletion of your data.

Can I hide my profile without deleting?
Yes. Pause your account in Settings. While paused you do not appear to other members, and your matches and messages are kept for when you return.

How do I get a copy of my data, or ask a question about it?
Email Ziba.dating@gmail.com. Depending on where you live, you may also have legal rights to access, correct, export, or delete your data, as described in the Privacy Policy.

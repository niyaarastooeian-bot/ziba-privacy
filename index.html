Ziba — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 12, 2026
Operator: Niayesh Arastooeian ("Ziba", "we", "us")
Contact: Ziba.dating@gmail.com

1. Who Ziba is for

Ziba is a dating and social-discovery app for adults. You must be 18 or older to use it. If you are under 18, do not use Ziba.

2. Information we collect

  • Account information: your email address and password (handled by our authentication provider), and your age/date of birth as required to confirm you are 18 or older. Ziba does not ask for or collect a phone number.
  • Verification: every member completes a face check inside the app before the account can be used: a selfie is compared against your profile photos to confirm they show the same person. Members may also complete an optional in-app ID check, which reads a government ID you show to your camera (IDs from all countries, including Iran, are accepted) and earns an "ID checked" badge. These checks run in-house; we do not send your ID or selfie to a third-party identity-verification company. We store your verification status, not a copy of your ID document.
  • Profile information: name, photos (up to 7), bio, prompts, height, current city, hometown, cultural background, faith and religiousness, intentions, relationship style, interests, languages, and the preferences you set. In the Social (friends) mode you may also add your job, a short description of your work, a work photo, and social handles (for example LinkedIn) — all optional.
  • Photo checks: profile photos are compared against your face-check selfie (face match) and screened by automated safety systems. Chat photos are screened before delivery; a photo that shows a person who is not wearing clothes is blocked.
  • Location: with your permission, your device location (When In Use) is used to show approximate distances ("X km away"). Your exact location is never shown to anyone.
  • Messages and media: chats, photos, and voice messages you send, in both Dating and Social modes.
  • Activity: likes, swipes (passes), matches, filters, blocks, and reports, plus basic device and diagnostic data needed to run and secure the app.
  • Purchases: subscriptions and like packs are processed by Apple. We receive confirmation of what you bought and your current tier; we never receive or store your card details.

3. How we use it

To run discovery and matching in your chosen mode (Dating or Social); to run the face check and optional ID check and prevent fake or underage accounts; to enforce our safety rules (photo screening, report handling, scam-pattern warnings in chat, moderation actions including warnings, suspension, and removal); to operate subscriptions and entitlements; and to debug, secure, and improve the app. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising or cross-app tracking. Ziba contains no third-party analytics or advertising trackers.

4. Who receives it

  • Other members see your profile as part of the service (profile information and photos you choose to publish, plus approximate distance).
  • Service providers process data only to run Ziba: hosting, database, and authentication (Supabase); email delivery for account verification codes (sent from Ziba.dating@gmail.com through Supabase's configured mail service); and Apple (payments and the App Store).
  • Moderation: if you are reported, the report, the profiles involved, and the relevant chat content may be reviewed by Ziba's operator to decide on moderation action.
  • Legal: we may disclose information when required by law or to respond to a valid safety report.

5. Retention

We keep your account and profile data — including your messages and your verification status — while your account is active. If you delete your account, that data is deleted within 30 days of deletion. Records of reports and moderation actions are kept for 90 days after deletion so we can enforce our safety rules and deal with repeat violations, and are then deleted. Data may be kept longer only where the law requires it.

6. Account deletion and your choices

You can delete your account from inside the app. Deleting locks the account immediately and then deletes it, subject to the retention rules in section 5 and legal obligations. You can also edit the profile fields the app exposes, manage the location permission in iOS Settings, block and report members, pause your account to hide your profile, and contact us at Ziba.dating@gmail.com to request access to or deletion of your data. Depending on where you live (for example the EU/UK or California), you may have additional rights to access, correct, export, or delete your data and to object to certain processing.

7. Photos you upload

Only upload photos you have the right to use. If you believe content on Ziba infringes your copyright, contact Ziba.dating@gmail.com with the details so we can review and, where appropriate, remove it.

8. Security

Access to member data is restricted by server-side policies so members can only access their own data; eligibility and verification status are recorded server-side and cannot be changed from the app. No system can guarantee it will never be breached; we work to minimize what we hold and who can reach it.

9. Changes and contact

We will announce material changes in the app or at this policy's URL before they take effect.

Ziba - Safety Center

How to stay safer on Ziba, what our checks really mean, and how to get help.

If you are in immediate danger, call 911 (in the US) or your local emergency number first. Then, when you can, report the person in the app or email us at Ziba.dating@gmail.com.

1. Staying safer while you chat

  • Keep the conversation in Ziba while you are getting to know someone. Moving to another app right away makes it harder for us to help if something goes wrong.
  • Never send money to someone you met on Ziba. Not as a loan, not as a gift, not for travel, not for an emergency. This includes gift cards, wire transfers, and cryptocurrency.
  • Never share financial details: bank accounts, card numbers, cash-app or payment handles for receiving money, or login codes sent to your phone or email.
  • Be careful with personal details early on: your home address, workplace, daily routine, and where your children go to school can all wait.
  • Trust slow starts. Someone who pushes for intense romance in the first days, or who always has a reason they cannot meet or video chat, is showing you a warning sign.

2. Scam rules: the patterns that are never real

These patterns are banned on Ziba, and a profile or chat showing them should be reported:

  • Asking for money, gift cards, crypto, or help with a financial transaction, for any reason, including a claimed emergency, a stuck shipment, a sick relative, or a business opportunity.
  • A story that keeps the person overseas: military service, oil rigs, aid work, or constant travel, always paired with a reason they cannot meet and a problem only money can solve.
  • Asking you to move money, receive and forward packages, or open accounts. That is money laundering, and it can make you part of a crime.
  • Links to investment groups, trading tips, or "guaranteed" returns.
  • Romance that escalates very fast, then turns into a request. Real interest does not arrive with an invoice.

Ziba shows scam-pattern warnings in chat for some of these patterns, but no warning system catches everything. If something feels off, stop, and report it. You will not be in trouble for reporting in good faith.

3. Meeting in person

  • Meet in a public place with other people around, at least the first few times.
  • Tell a friend who you are meeting, where, and when. Ziba has a Date Safety Check-in in the app, and you can share your date plans with a friend from inside the app. Use them.
  • Control your own ride. Drive yourself or use your own rideshare so you can leave whenever you want. Do not accept a pickup at your home.
  • Keep your phone charged and with you.
  • Watch your drink, and do not leave it unattended.
  • Leave if you feel uncomfortable. You do not owe anyone the rest of the evening, and you do not need a polite reason.

4. What Ziba's checks do, and what they do not prove

We state this plainly, because safety starts with honest expectations:

  • Ziba verifies every member's email address before the account exists. That proves control of an email inbox, nothing more.
  • The optional face check compares a selfie with a member's profile photos. It shows the photos appear to show the same person who took the selfie. It does not prove who that person is.
  • The optional ID check reads a government ID shown to the camera and adds an "ID checked" badge. The badge means the check was completed. We store the verification status, not a copy of the ID.
  • Ziba does not run criminal background checks on any member. No badge on Ziba means a person has a clean record, is safe, or is who they say they are.
  • Profile photos are screened by automated safety systems, and chat photos are screened before delivery; a photo that shows a person who is not wearing clothes is blocked. Screening catches a lot, not everything.
  • No app can make meeting strangers risk-free. Use the advice on this page, and trust your own judgment over any badge.

5. Reporting and blocking: how they work

  • Report from any profile, from inside any chat, or in Social. Pick the reason that fits and send it. You do not need to gather proof first.
  • When a report arrives, Ziba's operator reviews the report, the profiles involved, and the relevant chat content. A chat is opened for review only when it has been reported.
  • Outcomes follow the enforcement ladder in our Community Guidelines: a warning, content removal, suspension, or a ban, depending on what happened and whether it has happened before.
  • Block any member at any time. Blocking is immediate and two-way: you no longer see each other, anywhere in Ziba, and neither of you is told who blocked whom.
  • Reports made in bad faith, to harass someone or to settle a personal score, are themselves a violation of the Guidelines.
  • If a decision about your own account seems wrong, email Ziba.dating@gmail.com to appeal. A person reads appeals.

6. Crisis resources

  • Immediate danger: 911 (US), or your local emergency number.
  • 988 Suicide and Crisis Lifeline (US): call or text 988, free and available 24 hours.
  • Crisis Text Line (US): text HOME to 741741.
  • National Domestic Violence Hotline (US): call 1-800-799-7233 or text START to 88788.
  • If you are outside the US, your local emergency and crisis services can help; most countries have equivalents of the lines above.

7. Law enforcement

Ziba cooperates with law enforcement. We respond to valid legal requests for information, such as a subpoena or court order, and we will share information without a formal request where the law allows it and we believe in good faith that someone is in serious danger. If you are a law enforcement officer, contact Ziba.dating@gmail.com and state your agency and the nature of the request. If you have been the victim of a crime, report it to your local police as well as to us; our report helps us remove the person, and a police report is what starts an investigation.

8. Protecting your account

Member data is protected by encryption in transit and by server-side access rules, so members can only reach their own data. Keep your account safer: use an email account with a strong password and two-step verification turned on, never share your Ziba verification code with anyone (Ziba will never ask for it in chat), and email us if anything about your account looks wrong. Security questions and reports: Ziba.dating@gmail.com.

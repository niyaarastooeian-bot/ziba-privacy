Ziba - Community Guidelines

The rules every member agrees to. They apply in Dating and in Social, on profiles and in chats.

Ziba is a small community built on trust. These guidelines are the plain-language version of the rules behind the app's report reasons. Breaking them has consequences, described at the bottom of this page.

1. Be yourself

Your profile must be you: your real photos, your real answers, your own account. No fake profiles, no photos of other people, no profiles run by someone else, and no second accounts. One person, one account.

2. Be respectful

People on Ziba are looking for dates and friends, and everyone deserves to be treated decently. A "no", a slow reply, or an ended conversation is never a reason to be rude. If someone is not interested, accept it and move on.

3. No harassment or bullying

Do not insult, threaten, intimidate, stalk, or repeatedly contact someone who has stopped answering or asked you to stop. Do not send message after message to get a reaction. Harassment in chat is treated as seriously as harassment anywhere else.

4. No hate or discrimination

Do not attack or demean people because of who they are, including their background, faith, gender, sexuality, or appearance. Slurs and dehumanizing language are not allowed on profiles or in chats.

5. No sexual content in photos, and no unwanted sexual content

Profile photos must not contain nudity or sexual content. Chat photos are screened before delivery, and a photo that shows a person who is not wearing clothes is blocked. Do not send sexual content to someone who has not clearly agreed to receive it.

6. No solicitation or selling

Ziba is not a marketplace. Do not advertise, sell, recruit, promote a business or social account for commercial gain, or use your profile or chats to find customers.

7. No money requests, and no scams

Never ask another member for money, gift cards, cryptocurrency, financial help, or help moving money or packages, for any reason. Do not promote investments or trading schemes. Our Safety Center lists the common patterns. This rule has no exceptions for hard-luck stories.

8. No spam

Do not send bulk or repeated messages, copy-paste openers to everyone, or fill your profile or chats with links and promotions.

9. No impersonation

Do not pretend to be another person, a celebrity, a member of Ziba's team, or anyone you are not. Do not use someone else's photos.

10. No illegal activity

Do not use Ziba for anything illegal, including drugs, fraud, or arranging illegal services. Illegal content is removed and may be reported to law enforcement.

11. Adults only

Ziba is strictly 18+. No one under 18 may have an account, and no content involving a minor is ever allowed, in any form. Accounts and content that break this rule are removed immediately.

12. Respect other people's privacy

Do not share another person's private information, such as their phone number, address, workplace, or private messages, and do not post screenshots of private conversations to shame or expose someone. What is said in a chat stays in the chat, except when you report it to us for safety.

13. Honest reporting

Reports keep Ziba safe, and we read every one. Report behavior that actually breaks these guidelines. Making a false report, reporting someone to harass them, or reporting to settle a personal score is itself a violation, and can lead to the same consequences listed below.

What happens when a rule is broken

When a report is reviewed and a violation is confirmed, consequences follow a ladder, based on how serious the violation is and whether it has happened before:

  • Warning: we tell the member what rule was broken.
  • Content removal: the photo, answer, or message is taken down.
  • Suspension: the account is paused for a period of time.
  • Ban: the account is removed and the person may not return.

Serious violations skip the ladder: threats, scams, anything involving a minor, and repeat harassment can lead to an immediate ban. When your account is acted on, we tell you the reason.

Appeals

If you believe a decision about your account was wrong, email Ziba.dating@gmail.com with the email address on your account and what happened. A person reads every appeal, and we will tell you the outcome. Appealing does not guarantee the decision changes, but a real review does happen.
